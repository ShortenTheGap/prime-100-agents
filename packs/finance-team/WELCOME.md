You've added your **Finance Team** to Prime 100 OS. You don't need to open or edit anything in here, just talk to the team in the app and they'll take it from there.

## Who's on your team

- **Charlie Munger**: your CFO, a working reconstruction of the investor and Berkshire Hathaway vice chairman. He handles cash flow and runway, margins and unit economics, pricing economics, whether a hire, a spend, or a deal is worth it, comp and commission plans, and reviews of your P&L and forecasts. This team grows over time. As we release new finance specialists, they'll show up here automatically on your next update.

## How to use it

1. In Prime 100 OS, open **Meet Your Team** and pick **Charlie**.
2. Tell him the decision or the number that's on your mind, in your own words.
3. He'll ask what he needs to know, then do the work and hand it back for your review. He never moves money, and nothing gets sent or published without your say-so.

## For the sharpest results

Charlie reads your team's `shared/company-context.md` (your business, offers, and prices) at the start of every session, plus any financial exports you keep in your Business Brain. The more accurate those are, the sharper his numbers.

## "I only see a couple of files, where's everything else?"

That's normal. The team's working files live in folders your computer keeps **hidden** by default (that's just how technical support files work). Everything is there and running, you interact with the team by chatting in the app, not by opening files. On a Mac you can press **Cmd + Shift + .** in Finder to reveal hidden files if you're curious.
