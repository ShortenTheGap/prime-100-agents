# ✅ Your Operations Team is installed

You've added your **Operations Team** to Prime 100 OS. You don't need to open or edit anything in here, just talk to the team in the app and they'll take it from there.

## Who's on your team

- **Peter Drucker**: your Operations Manager, a working reconstruction of the management thinker. He handles priorities and focus, quarterly objectives, where your week is going, time audits, delegation, roles and hiring, processes and SOPs, meeting cadences, and what to stop doing. If it's about running the business effectively, it's his.

This team grows over time. As we release new operations specialists, they'll show up here automatically on your next update.

## How to use it

1. In Prime 100 OS, open **Meet Your Team** and pick **Peter**.
2. Tell him what you want to get done, or where your time is going, in your own words.
3. He'll ask what he needs to know, then do the work and hand it back for your review. Nothing gets sent, scheduled, or published without your say-so.

## For the sharpest results

Peter reads your team's `shared/company-context.md` (your business, customers, and priorities) and `shared/brand-voice.md` (how you sound) at the start of every session. The more accurate those are, the sharper his advice.

## "I only see a couple of files, where's everything else?"

That's normal. The team's working files live in folders your computer keeps **hidden** by default (that's just how technical support files work). Everything is there and running, you interact with the team by chatting in the app, not by opening files. On a Mac you can press **Cmd + Shift + .** in Finder to reveal hidden files if you're curious.
