You've added your **Marketing Team** to Prime 100 OS. You don't need to open or edit anything in here, just talk to the team in the app and they'll take it from there.

## How to use it

1. In Prime 100 OS, set the folder selector at the top to **my-ai-marketing-team**.
2. Tell the team what you're selling or promoting, in your own words.
3. They'll ask what they need to know, then do the work and hand it back for your review. Nothing gets sent or published without your say-so.

## For the sharpest results

David reads two files at the start of every session: `shared/company-context.md` (your business, audience, and offers) and `shared/brand-voice.md` (how you sound). The more accurate those are, the more on-brand his work. He'll offer to help you fill them in if they're still blank.

## "I only see a couple of files, where's everything else?"

That's normal. The team's working files live in folders your computer keeps **hidden** by default (that's just how technical support files work). Everything is there and running, you interact with the team by chatting in the app, not by opening files. On a Mac you can press **Cmd + Shift + .** in Finder to reveal hidden files if you're curious.
