# Stage 2 Reference: The Stand Challenger

## What This Stage Produces

A Stand File saved as `02_stand-file.md`. A Stand is a contrarian point
of view that meets three criteria:

1. **Contrarian** (against common wisdom in their space)
2. **True** (they believe it and can defend it)
3. **Has receipts** (they've done it or helped a client do it)

The Stand is what the operator will say that nobody else in their space
will say.

## Required Before Starting

The operator must have a locked `01_ideal-buyer-persona.md`. The Stand
gets calibrated to a specific buyer. If the Persona doesn't exist, send
them back to Stage 1.

Open the Stand File session by loading the Persona's one-sentence
compression and confirming it still holds.

## The 6 Challenge Lenses

Run the operator through these lenses in sequence, one at a time. Each
lens surfaces contrarian thinking a different way. Don't skip lenses.

### Lens 1: Inversion
Ask: "What's the most common advice given in your space right now?
What would happen if you did the exact opposite?"

Look for: Advice that's universally given but rarely questioned.

### Lens 2: Paradox
Ask: "What's something that should be true based on conventional
wisdom but isn't true in your experience?"

Look for: Gaps between theory and reality the operator has noticed.

### Lens 3: Dirty Secret
Ask: "What does everyone in your space privately know but won't say
publicly?"

Look for: Industry omertà. The thing peers acknowledge in private DMs
but never post.

### Lens 4: Sacred Cow
Ask: "What's a 'must-have' in your space that's actually optional or
wrong?"

Look for: Best practices that became dogma without evidence.

### Lens 5: Unnamed Thing
Ask: "What's a phenomenon you've noticed in your space that nobody has
named yet?"

Look for: Patterns the operator can see but hasn't articulated.

### Lens 6: Reframe
Ask: "What's something your space calls a 'problem' that's actually a
feature? Or a 'feature' that's actually a problem?"

Look for: Labels that obscure the truth.

## The Temperature Test

After the 6 lenses, the operator should have 3-5 candidate Stands. For
each candidate, run the temperature test.

Ask the operator to read the Stand out loud (or react to it in writing).
Score it:

- **HOT**: They get angry, lean forward, gestures get bigger. This is a
  real Stand. Lock it.
- **WARM**: They believe it but it sounds like something they'd say in
  a polite room. Push harder. Run more lenses.
- **COLD**: They're stating a fact, not a Stand. Strip it.

A Stand isn't a Stand until it scores HOT.

## The Receipts Test

Every HOT Stand must have receipts. Ask:

1. When did you first realize this?
2. What specific moment in your business or a client's business proved
   it?
3. What number or named example can you point to?

If the operator can't produce receipts, the Stand stays in draft. A
Stand without receipts is just an opinion. A Stand with receipts is a
worldview.

## Output Structure

`02_stand-file.md` opens with one line: "This Stand File is calibrated
to [the ideal buyer](./01_ideal-buyer-persona.md)." Then:

### Stand #1 (Primary)
**The Stand:** [One sentence, contrarian, defensible]
**The world believes:** [Common wisdom]
**I believe:** [Contrarian POV]
**My proof:** [Specific client, number, or moment]
**Temperature:** HOT
**Use this Stand for:** [Specific content angles where this Stand
applies]

### Stand #2 (Secondary)
[Same structure]

### Stand #3 (Future)
[Same structure, optional]

## Stage 2 Rules

1. Never accept a WARM or COLD Stand. Push until it goes HOT.
2. Every HOT Stand must have receipts.
3. Don't invent Stands. The operator must believe it.
4. A Stand without a specific moment that proved it isn't a Stand yet.
5. Limit the Stand File to 3 entries max. More than 3 dilutes focus.
6. If after running all 6 lenses the operator can't produce a HOT
   Stand with receipts, flag for escalation per SKILL.md.
7. Save with the frontmatter and relative-markdown-link format defined in SKILL.md.
