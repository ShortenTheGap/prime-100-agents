# Next test template

Use this structure for `Avatar/Offer Next Test.md` in the main team. One test at a time: the most important unproven assumption, not a research programme.

```
# Next Test: [offer name]
Written [date] for Offer v[X.Y]

## The assumption
[The single most important thing that is unproven. One sentence, stated so it could be
wrong.]

## Who should test it
[The people who match the Specific Person. Say how to tell a target prospect from a
non target one, because feedback from outside the target answers a different question.]

## What to show them
[The exact asset or conversation. Nothing else changes at the same time.]

## What to ask for
[One action.]

## What to watch
[What counts as a result, and what you will be able to tell from it.]

## How to read the outcome
- If [outcome A]: [what that suggests, and what it still does not prove]
- If [outcome B]: [same]
- If [outcome C]: [same]

## Known limits
[Sample size, who was reached, what was not tested.]
```

## Rules

- Purchases, deposits, and qualified calls booked are stronger evidence than compliments or hypothetical interest. Say so in the file when the owner proposes a softer test.
- Never claim statistical certainty from a small test. A handful of conversations is a signal, not proof.
- One variable at a time, or the result cannot be read.
- An internal critique, including yours, is never a substitute for this test.
