# Offer review template

Use this structure for `Avatar/Offer Review.md` in the main team. It is for the owner, not for a buyer. Still no em dashes and no en dashes.

```
# Offer Review: [offer name]
Reviewed [date] against Offer v[X.Y] | Living Avatar v[X.Y] | MOAT v[X.Y]
[Say here if Living Avatar or MOAT is missing, and what that means for this review.]

## The three biggest issues
### 1. [Problem in one sentence]
- Why it matters: [what it costs the business or the buyer]
- Recommended change: [specific enough to act on]
- Trade off: [what gets worse or what it costs]
- Still needed: [the evidence to collect, or the decision the owner has to make]
### 2. ...
### 3. ...

## Full findings
### Pass A: will they want it?
| Check | Rating | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| ... | clear / weak / unknown | ... |
### Pass B: will they believe it?
[same table]
### Pass C: can we deliver it as it grows?
[same table]

## Scale problems
[One entry per problem.]
- What breaks: [the specific thing]
- Roughly when: [client count or hours, or "not known from what I have"]
- Why it matters: [consequence]

[Then, in plain words: this offer needs a scale plan before you grow it. Your next step
is to ask David to loop in Manny, the AI dev agent, to help solve it, or bring it to Sue.
Name no hires, no tools, and no new delivery model.]

## Economics run
[Only when the calculation script was run. The scenario table, then the assumptions and
unknowns exactly as the script reported them. Contribution is before overhead and tax and
is never called net profit. Inputs are saved in Avatar/Offer Working Notes.md.]

## Conflicts with MOAT
[Only when MOAT exists and the offer work contradicts it. Name the MOAT section, say what
conflicts, and recommend re-running MOAT. Never edit MOAT.]

## Decisions I need from you
1. [One decision per line, each one answerable]

## What I did not do
[Anything outside this skill: a landing page, a sales pitch, a deck, a VSL script, or the
fix for a scale problem.]

## Next
Run MOAT to evaluate this offer, then Buyer Runway for the 30 days after someone opts in.
```

Internal reviews are labeled as internal reviews. Nothing in this file is buyer validation.
