# The 7P Interview Framework — Complete Question Set

Work through these 7 sections sequentially. Ask one question at a time within each section. Push for specificity. After completing each P, summarize what you've captured.

---

## 1. PERSON (Avatar/Who)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- Who specifically do you help? (Push beyond "coaches" or "service businesses" — get the real, specific person)
- What stage are they at? (Revenue, team size, growth phase)
- How do they see themselves? What personality traits define them?
- What are their aspirations? What do they really want and WHY?
- What are their secret struggles — the 3am ceiling-staring fears they don't talk about publicly?
- How does your story connect to theirs? (Were you them, or have you coached many like them?)

**Quality check:** If they say "coaches who want more leads," push back: "Let's get more specific. What KIND of coaches? What industry? What revenue level? Paint me a picture of ONE person."

**Capture:** Specific who, stage details, personality, aspirations, secret struggles, your connection to them.

---

## 2. PROBLEM (The Gap)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- What was their original hope or dream when they started?
- What's happening instead? What's their current reality?
- What are the pointable, visible problems they face daily? (Things you can see, touch, measure)
- What's the emotional cost? What does this feel like for them?
- What metaphor describes their current state? (Hamster wheel? Roller coaster? Sinking ship?)

**Quality check:** Push for visceral, movie-scene detail: "Show me what this looks like on a Tuesday afternoon. What's happening? How does it feel?"

**Capture:** Original dream, current reality, pointable problems, emotional cost, metaphor.

---

## 3. PROOF (Social Proof & Case Studies)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- List 5–10 clients who got great results (first names are fine)
- Who almost didn't join because money was tight, but got amazing results anyway?
- Who almost didn't join because time was an issue, but succeeded anyway?
- For each pillar of your model/framework, give me ONE killer case study: Name + where they started → where they ended up. What emotional detail makes this story pop?

**Quality check:** If stories lack emotion or specifics: "What was at stake for them? What would have happened if they didn't solve this? What did they say when they got the result?"

**Capture:** Client names list, money objection story, time objection story, pillar-specific case studies with before/after and emotional hooks.

---

## 4. POISON (Shared Enemy/Bad Medicine)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- What advice do they THINK they're supposed to follow? (List all the "shoulds")
- Why is that advice wrong or incomplete?
- What's a metaphor that describes this broken system or bad advice?
- Complete this sentence: "It's not your fault this hasn't worked because..."

**Quality check:** Make sure they're critiquing SYSTEMS/APPROACHES, not naming specific people. Push for a strong metaphor that makes the bad advice memorable.

**Capture:** Bad advice list, why it's wrong, metaphor, "not your fault" statement.

---

## 5. PHILOSOPHY (Mission & Beliefs)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- Complete this: "My mission is to help [WHO] achieve [WHAT]"
- Complete this: "I believe you deserve a way to [RESULT] without [PAIN/SACRIFICE]"

**Quality check:** If it sounds generic: "Why does this matter to YOU personally? What fires you up about this?"

**Capture:** Mission statement, belief statement.

---

## 6. PLAN (Your Model/Framework)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- What's your model called? (The [Something] Plan/Framework/System)
- What's the dream result in the center? (The main transformation)
- What are the 3 main pillars/parts/phases?
- For EACH pillar:
  - What is it and what's the benefit?
  - What are 2–3 key steps/levers within it?
  - What's the benefit of each step?
  - Remind me of the client story that proves this pillar works

**Quality check:** This needs the most detail. If they rush: "Let's make sure someone watching your VSL truly understands HOW this works. Walk me through pillar 1 like I'm your ideal client."

**Capture:** Model name, dream result, 3 pillars with benefits, steps with benefits, proof story per pillar.

---

## 7. PRIZE (Call to Action)

**Questions (one at a time):**
- What's the next step you want them to take? (Book a call? Read a doc? Buy something?)
- What happens in that next step? (Outline the experience)
- How do you make it feel safe/low-pressure?
- What's the real scarcity or reason to act now?
- What's the exact action? (Click here, find a time, send a message with the word X...)

**Quality check:** The CTA must be crystal clear and friction-free: "If I'm watching your VSL right now and I'm excited, what's the ONE thing I do next?"

**Capture:** Next step, what happens, safety element, scarcity, exact action.
