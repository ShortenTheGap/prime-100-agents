# Headline Generation & Scoring Protocol

Use these patterns, trigger words, and rules when scoring headlines and generating improvements. Headlines that match these proven structures consistently outperform generic alternatives.

---

## Primary Viral Hook Patterns

### 1. The Wealth & Success Pattern

**Structure:** "[Time/Status Indicator] + [Transformation Promise] + [Credibility Element]"

**Examples:**
- "13 Years of No BS Business Advice in 79 Mins"
- "How to Get SO Rich You Question the Meaning of Making Money"
- "If I Wanted to Go From $0 to $1M in 12 Months, Here's What I'd Do"

**Markers:** Specific timeframes, wealth indicators, authenticity markers ("No BS," "Brutally Honest").

### 2. The Percentage-Based Exclusivity Pattern

**Structure:** "[Large Percentage] + [Knowledge Gap] + [Specific Domain]"

**Examples:**
- "99% Don't Know These Tips and Tricks"
- "8 Habits That Will Fix 98% of Your Problems"
- "13 Hacks to be 99.9% More Disciplined"

**Markers:** Percentages above 95%, knowledge gaps, exclusive information framing.

### 3. The Life-Changing Transformation Pattern

**Structure:** "[Command Word] + [Pain Point] + [Number] + [Solution]"

**Examples:**
- "Stop Wasting Your Life - 7 Things I Quit to Go From Broke to Millionaire"
- "Watch This if You're Tired of Being Broke"
- "REPROGRAM Your Mind to be Rich in 22 Minutes"

**Markers:** Action verbs opening, specific pain points, clear transformation promise.

### 4. The Authority/Elite Pattern

**Structure:** "[Number] + [Topic] + [Elite Reference]"

**Examples:**
- "7 High Income Skills of the Top 1%"
- "The 21 Principles of the Top 0.01%"
- "$100M CEO Explains How to Build A Brand"

**Markers:** Elite group references, specific numbers, status indicators.

---

## High-Performing Trigger Words

**Transformation:** "From," "Become," "Transform," "Turn," "Go From"
**Urgency:** "Now," "Before," "Watch This," "Stop," "Today"
**Authority:** "Ultimate," "Best," "Top," "Proven," "#1"
**Authenticity:** "Brutally Honest," "No BS," "Actually," "The Truth About"
**Curiosity:** "Secret," "Hidden," "Nobody Talks About," "What No One Tells You"
**Exclusivity:** "Only," "Few," "Elite," "Inner Circle"

---

## Numerical Optimization

- Prefer odd numbers (7, 13, 21) — they outperform even numbers
- Use specific timeframes (22 minutes, 79 mins, 90 days) — not rounded
- Include high percentages (98%, 99.9%) — creates exclusivity
- Dollar amounts with specificity ($100M, $10K) — outperform "lots of money"
- Place numbers at the start when possible — catches the eye first

---

## Emotional Trigger Map

**Primary triggers (highest impact):**
- FOMO — "99% don't know..." / "Before it's too late..."
- Curiosity — "The truth about..." / "What no one tells you..."
- Hope — "How to go from... to..." / "The blueprint for..."

**Secondary triggers (amplifiers):**
- Urgency — "You have 30 days..." / "Right now..."
- Exclusivity — "Top 1%..." / "Only a few..."
- Authority — "$100M CEO..." / "World-class..."

---

## Format Optimization

- **Length:** Under 60 characters when possible (especially for email subjects and YouTube)
- **Capitalization:** Capitalize 1–2 key words maximum for emphasis (not ALL CAPS throughout)
- **Numbers:** Include at the start when possible
- **Punctuation:** Avoid multiple exclamation points. One is fine. Zero is often better.
- **Emojis:** Limit to 0–1 in titles. Platform-dependent.

---

## Prohibited Patterns

- Multiple exclamation points ("Amazing Tips!!!")
- Clickbait without substance (promise must be deliverable)
- Excessive emoji chains
- Vague generalities ("Great tips for success")
- Starting with "How to" when a stronger pattern exists
- Passive constructions ("Things that can be learned")

---

## Platform-Specific Notes

**YouTube titles:** Under 60 chars, front-load keywords, capitalize strategically, numbers perform well.

**Email subject lines:** Under 50 chars ideal, personalization tokens help, avoid spam trigger words ("free," "guarantee," "act now"), test with/without emoji.

**Social media hooks:** First line must stop the scroll. Pair with strong visual. Can be slightly longer if the hook is strong enough.

**Sales page headlines:** Can be longer. Focus on the transformation promise. Include the audience and the result.

## Scoring Reference Examples

**Score 9/10:** "99% Don't Know These Woodworking Tips and Tricks"
*Why:* Strong FOMO with "99%," promises exclusive knowledge, clear benefit. Nearly perfect — could be tighter by naming one standout tip.

**Score 8/10:** "How to Make Money So Fast It Feels ILLEGAL"
*Why:* "ILLEGAL" sparks emotion and urgency, teasing a secret shortcut. Slight risk of spam filters — swap "ILLEGAL" for "UNFAIR" to boost deliverability.

**Score 9/10:** "If I Wanted to Go From $0 to $1M in 12 Months, Here's What I'd Do"
*Why:* Clear transformation, specific timeframe, and first-person authority create credibility and curiosity. Sharpen by trimming "I Wanted to" for faster momentum.
