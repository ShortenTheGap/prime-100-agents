# Viral Shorts Playbook (Mode 2)

The complete method for a high-retention, ~90 second Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts script with editing direction. Every script has 5 components: a scroll-stopping hook, a fast body broken into slides, editing and visual direction throughout, an engagement booster, and a natural CTA.

---

## Inputs

1. **Niche or industry** (for example: AI, business growth, personal development)
2. **Target audience** (who the video is for)
3. **Main message** (for example: AI mistakes, sales hacks, productivity tips)
4. **Engagement goal** (comments, shares, follows, saves)
5. **Tone** (educational, funny, dramatic, relatable)
6. **Editing style** (fast-paced, cinematic, meme-heavy, minimalistic)
7. **Call to action** (for example: "Follow for more!" or "DM me 'HACK' for the full guide")

If the member gives only a topic, make reasonable assumptions, write the script now, then offer to refine.

---

## Component 1: Hook (First 3 Seconds)

The hook MUST stop the scroll. Use one of these proven formulas:

- **Challenge a belief:** "Everything you've heard about [topic] is WRONG."
- **Tease an outcome:** "By the end of this, you'll NEVER struggle with [problem] again."
- **Disruptive question:** "Why is NOBODY talking about this?"
- **Storytelling opener:** "I lost $10,000 because I made this mistake..."
- **Create urgency:** "You have exactly 30 days to fix this..."

NEVER use generic hooks like "Hey guys, today I'm gonna show you...", "Here are some tips on...", or "Want to learn how to...?"

Include editing direction for the hook (zoom, sound effect, visual element).

---

## Component 2: Body (High-Retention Main Content)

Break the content into short, punchy slides or segments to prevent drop-off. Put a twist at the halfway point.

**Educational / Revealing structure:**
- Slide 1: "I bet you're making this mistake..."
- Slide 2: "Here's why it's killing your progress."
- Slide 3: "Instead, do THIS..."
- Slide 4: "Look at the difference when you fix it."
- Slide 5: "Try this TODAY and see results immediately."

**Personal Storytelling structure:**
- Slide 1: "I almost gave up on [goal] because..."
- Slide 2: "Then, I discovered this secret..."
- Slide 3: "Here's exactly what I did..."
- Slide 4: "The results? INSANE."
- Slide 5: "Don't make my mistake. Do this instead."

Use listicle formats ("3 things you NEED to know...") and state the number upfront to open a loop.

---

## Component 3: Editing and Visual Strategy

Put specific editing notes throughout the script:

- **Jump cuts and fast pacing:** keep energy high
- **Bold text overlays and captions:** reinforce key messages visually
- **Sound effects and background music:** create a dynamic feel
- **Zoom-ins and camera movement:** add intensity to key moments
- **B-roll footage or memes:** add humor and visual variety
- **A twist or surprise in the middle:** keep people engaged

Format editing notes in brackets: [Jump cut to reaction], [Add boom sound effect], [Insert meme], [Zoom in on face], [Text overlay: "THIS IS KEY"]

---

## Component 4: Engagement Booster

A question or challenge that drives comments and shares:

- "What do YOU think? Drop a comment if this makes sense!"
- "Which side are you on? #1 or #2?"
- "Have you tried this before? Comment YES or NO."
- "Tag someone who needs to hear this."

---

## Component 5: Call to Action

It must feel natural and compelling. Match it to the engagement goal:

- **Follow CTA:** "Follow for more secrets like this!"
- **DM CTA:** "DM me '[KEYWORD]' for the full breakdown."
- **Save CTA:** "Save this so you don't forget!"
- **Share CTA:** "Send this to a friend who needs to hear this."

---

## Output Format

```
HOOK (First 3 Seconds):
[Verbal hook line]
[Editing direction in brackets]

BODY (Main Content):
1. [Slide/segment with editing notes]
2. [Slide/segment with editing notes]
3. [Slide/segment with editing notes]
4. [Slide/segment with editing notes]
5. [Slide/segment with editing notes]

ENGAGEMENT BOOSTER:
[Question or challenge]

CTA:
[Call to action]

PRODUCTION NOTES:
- Tone: [As specified]
- Editing style: [As specified]
- Key emphasis moments: [What to hit hardest]
- Music suggestion: [Genre/vibe]
```

---

## Rules

- Never produce generic, boring scripts
- Every hook must stop the scroll in 3 seconds
- Body content is broken into punchy segments
- Editing and visual direction appear throughout
- Match the member's tone and editing style
- Keep the total script to ~90 seconds when spoken

## After Delivering

Ask: "Want me to create variations with different hooks or a different structure? I can also adjust the tone, editing style, or CTA."
