# Viral Hooks Formula Library

Proven hook formulas for short-form video content. Replace [blank] with your specific topic, audience, problem, or solution. Choose hooks that create curiosity, highlight problems, or promise valuable insights.

When generating scripts, select hooks from DIFFERENT categories to give the user variety to test.

---

## Problem-Focused Hooks

- "This is why your [blank] isn't giving you results"
- "Stop wasting time on [blank]"
- "The biggest mistake I see [audience] making is..."
- "What everyone gets wrong about [blank]"
- "The real reason everyone is [doing something]"
- "Why [blank] is more important than you think"
- "The biggest lie in [industry/topic]"

## Curiosity-Driven Hooks

- "Only a few people realize [blank]"
- "What no one tells you about [blank]"
- "The secret to [achieving a specific result]"
- "What I wish I had known about [blank]"
- "The truth about [common belief]"
- "This [blank] strategy is a game changer"
- "This could change the way you think about [blank]"

## Transformation Hooks

- "How I transformed [blank] in [time frame]"
- "Why I stopped [doing something]"
- "I used to think [blank], but I was wrong"
- "How [blank] can be your biggest game-changer"
- "Why [blank] is the best decision I ever made"

## List & Number Hooks

- "[Number] proven methods to [achieve a goal]"
- "[Number] habits of highly effective [type of people]"
- "[Number] things every [type of person] should know"
- "The [number] types of [people] in [industry/niche]"
- "[Blank] explained in [number] simple steps"

## Instructional Hooks

- "How to overcome [a common problem]"
- "How to make [blank] work for you"
- "How to guarantee [desired result]"
- "The best way to handle [a situation/problem]"
- "How to stop [a bad habit] and start [a good habit]"
- "How to be a [blank] in a world full of [blank]"

## Comparison Hooks

- "[Blank] vs. [blank]: What's really better?"
- "[Blank]: Expectations vs. Reality"
- "What [blank] really looks like"
- "The best and worst of [blank]"

## Contrarian Hooks

- "[Blank] doesn't work unless you [do something specific]"
- "Why it's time to rethink [common practice]"
- "[Blank] debunked: what really works"
- "Why we need to talk about [blank]"

## Best/Ultimate Hooks

- "The best [blank] I've ever used"
- "The ultimate cheat sheet on [blank]"
- "The single most effective strategy for [blank]"
- "[Blank] tips that are actually worth trying"

## Conditional Hooks

- "Before you [do something], read this"
- "If you're still [doing something], here's why you should stop"
- "If you only [do one thing], make sure it's [blank]"
- "What happens when you [do something]"

## Learning/Insight Hooks

- "What [industry/profession] can learn from [another industry/profession]"
- "How avoiding [blank] can help you [achieve something]"
- "Why [blank] is underrated"
- "[Blank] might be your best [opportunity/tool]"

---

## Hook Testing Strategy

For each video, create 3 hooks using DIFFERENT formula categories:
- Hook 1: Problem-focused or Contrarian
- Hook 2: Curiosity-driven or Transformation
- Hook 3: Number/List-based or Conditional

This gives variety to test what resonates with the specific audience.

## Customization by Audience

- **B2B audiences:** Professional language, focus on ROI and results
- **B2C audiences:** Emotional language, focus on transformation and benefits
- **Coaches/consultants:** "You" language, focus on client problems
- **Service providers:** Specific industry terms, focus on pain points
- **Product businesses:** Outcome language, focus on before/after
