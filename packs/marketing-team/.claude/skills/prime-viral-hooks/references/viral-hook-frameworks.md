# Viral YouTube Hook Frameworks — Reference Guide

This file contains proven viral hook structures extracted from top-performing YouTube videos across multiple niches. Use these as inspiration to craft original hooks tailored to the user's specific audience and offer. Never copy hooks verbatim — adapt the structure and pattern to the user's niche.

## How to Use This Reference

Each section below shows a hook framework, explains why it works, and provides examples from different niches to show the pattern. When generating hooks for a user, mix and match these frameworks and adapt them to the user's specific market language, metrics, and pain points.

---

## Framework 1: The "X% Don't Know" Hook

**Pattern:** "99% of [audience] don't know [this thing]"

**Why it works:** Creates instant FOMO. The reader thinks "Am I in the 99%?" and needs to find out. The high percentage implies hidden, exclusive knowledge.

**Examples across niches:**
- "99% Don't Know These Woodworking Tips and Tricks"
- "99% of Beginners Don't Know These 5 Mistakes Ruin Wood!"
- "99% of Beginners Don't Know the Basics of Router Bits"

**Adaptation notes:** Replace the skill/topic with the user's niche. Works especially well with "mistakes," "tips," "basics," and "secrets."

---

## Framework 2: The "If I Had to Start Over" Hook

**Pattern:** "If I wanted to [achieve goal] in [timeframe], here's what I'd do"

**Why it works:** Implies the creator has already achieved the goal and is sharing the shortcut. Creates a "blueprint" expectation. The conditional framing ("if I wanted to") feels authentic rather than braggy.

**Examples:**
- "If I Wanted to Become a Millionaire In 2024, This is What I'd Do [FULL BLUEPRINT]"
- "If I Wanted to Go From $0 to $1M in 12 Months, Here's What I'd Do"
- "If I Were Single and Starting Over, This Is What I Would Do"

**Adaptation notes:** Swap in the user's specific goal and audience. Adding "[FULL BLUEPRINT]" or "EXACT plan" increases click-through.

---

## Framework 3: The "Brutally Honest Advice" Hook

**Pattern:** "X years of [topic] advice in Y minutes"

**Why it works:** Implies massive value compression. The viewer gets decades of wisdom in a short time. "Brutally honest" and "no BS" signal authenticity.

**Examples:**
- "13 Years Of Brutally Honest Business Advice in 90 Mins"
- "13 Years of No BS Business Advice in 79 Mins"
- "Brutally Honest Advice to My Younger Poorer Self"
- "14 Life Lessons I Wish I Knew Earlier"

**Adaptation notes:** Works best when the creator has significant experience. The year count adds credibility. Can also use "X things I wish I knew" variant.

---

## Framework 4: The "Feels Illegal" Hook

**Pattern:** "How to [do thing] so fast it feels ILLEGAL"

**Why it works:** The word "illegal" is a massive pattern interrupt. It implies a shortcut or loophole that's almost too good to be true. Creates intense curiosity about what the method is.

**Examples:**
- "How to Make Money So Fast It Feels ILLEGAL"
- "These AI Tools Feel ILLEGAL (But They Work Like Magic)"

**Adaptation notes:** Works for any niche where there's a surprisingly easy or fast method. The parenthetical "(But They Work Like Magic)" adds a second curiosity layer.

---

## Framework 5: The "Get Ahead of X%" Hook

**Pattern:** "How to get ahead of X% of [people]"

**Why it works:** Taps into competitive drive and status anxiety. Nobody wants to be average. The specific percentage makes it feel data-driven.

**Examples:**
- "How to Get Ahead of 99% of People"
- "It's Actually Pretty Easy to Get Ahead of 99% of People"
- "The 21 Principles of the Top 0.01%"
- "7 High Income Skills of the Top 1%"

**Adaptation notes:** Can focus on getting ahead of competitors, peers, or the general population. The "it's actually pretty easy" variant adds a contrarian element.

---

## Framework 6: The "Watch This If" Hook

**Pattern:** "Watch this if you're [relatable negative state]"

**Why it works:** Self-selection. The viewer identifies with the state and feels the video was made specifically for them. Creates an emotional connection before they even click.

**Examples:**
- "watch this if you're tired of being broke"
- "I'm 44. If You're 20 or 30, Watch This"

**Adaptation notes:** The negative state should match the user's audience's primary frustration. Lowercase, casual formatting can increase authenticity feel.

---

## Framework 7: The "Top X / List" Hook

**Pattern:** "Top X [things] that [surprising outcome]"

**Why it works:** Lists are inherently clickable. The brain wants to know all X items. Adding a surprising outcome ("that sell," "that are killing your business") adds curiosity.

**Examples:**
- "Top 5 Woodworking Projects That Sell"
- "5 Tools Under $30 Every Woodworker Needs"
- "Top 13 Pocket Hole Tips and Tricks | ULTIMATE Guide To Become a Pro"

**Adaptation notes:** Specific numbers outperform round numbers (13 > 10). Adding "ULTIMATE Guide" or "FULL LIST" implies completeness.

---

## Framework 8: The "Wealth Killers / Mistakes" Hook

**Pattern:** "The X [thing] killers that no one talks about"

**Why it works:** Implies hidden danger — something is sabotaging the viewer's results without their knowledge. "No one talks about" adds exclusivity.

**Examples:**
- "The 7 Wealth Killers That No One Talks About"
- "Avoid These 7 AI Automation Mistakes That Are Killing Your Profits"
- "99% of Beginner's Don't Know These Table Saw Mistakes to Avoid!"

**Adaptation notes:** Works for any niche. Replace "wealth" with whatever metric matters to the audience (leads, conversions, health, relationships).

---

## Framework 9: The "Hater's Guide" Hook

**Pattern:** "The Hater's Guide to [brand/topic] | What to Buy and Avoid"

**Why it works:** Contrarian and opinionated. Promises an honest, unfiltered review. "Hater's Guide" is a pattern interrupt that signals the creator won't sugarcoat.

**Examples:**
- "The Hater's Guide to Harbor Freight | What to Avoid"
- "The Hater's Guide to Ryobi Tools | What To Buy and Avoid"

**Adaptation notes:** Works for tool reviews, platform comparisons, methodology critiques. The format implies insider knowledge and strong opinions.

---

## Framework 10: The "Proof It's Not Luck" Hook

**Pattern:** "I did [impressive thing] X times to prove it's not luck"

**Why it works:** Addresses the viewer's skepticism head-on. If the creator did it repeatedly, it must be a system — not luck. Creates curiosity about the system.

**Examples:**
- "I Built 4 Businesses In A Row To Show It's Not Luck"
- "I Taught 100 Kids How to Make More Money Than Their Parents"

**Adaptation notes:** Requires some form of repeatable proof. The specific number adds credibility.

---

## Framework 11: The "Rules/Habits/Principles" Hook

**Pattern:** "X rules/habits that [transformed result]"

**Why it works:** Frameworks and rules feel systematic and actionable. The viewer believes they can replicate the results by following the same rules.

**Examples:**
- "These Rules Made Me So Rich I Questioned the Meaning of Money"
- "40 Brutal Truths I Wish I Knew in My 20s"
- "8 Habits That Will Fix 98% of Your Problems"
- "Stop Wasting Your Life - 7 Things I Quit to Go From Broke to Millionaire"
- "13 Hacks to be 99.9% More Disciplined That Cost Nothing"

**Adaptation notes:** Works for any niche. The more specific the result ("fix 98% of your problems"), the more clickable.

---

## Framework 12: The "Lazy/Contrarian" Hook

**Pattern:** Challenges the conventional hustle narrative with a contrarian take.

**Why it works:** Pattern interrupt. Everyone says "work harder." Saying the opposite stops the scroll and creates curiosity about the alternative approach.

**Examples:**
- "If you want to be rich, be lazy."
- "you can build your dream life in 12 months, here's how…"

**Adaptation notes:** Works best when the creator can back up the contrarian claim with a real method or framework. Lowercase, casual tone adds authenticity.

---

## Power Words to Incorporate

Use these throughout hooks to increase click-through:

**Urgency/Exclusivity:** secret, hidden, nobody talks about, illegal, banned, leaked, insider
**Scale/Impact:** ultimate, insane, shocking, game-changing, revolutionary
**Specificity:** exact, step-by-step, full blueprint, complete guide, word-for-word
**Proof/Authority:** proven, tested, I built, I generated, X years of
**Emotion:** brutal, honest, painful, terrifying, life-changing
**Numbers:** Always use specific numbers over round ones (13 > 10, 97% > "most")
