# Rhetorical Frames Reference

Use these frames when writing hooks, headlines, subheads, section openers, and pattern interrupts. Match the frame to the page goal — see "Frame-to-Page Matching" at the bottom.

> **Examples are illustrative only.** Health, money, and earnings lines here show the technique, not claims to reuse. Every claim in the member's copy must be true and provable, and David flags health, financial, and earnings claims to the member before drafting.

## 1. CURIOSITY
Highlights a tiny, interesting gap between the prospect and their desired outcome.

### Information-Gap
- **Paradoxical Question (Known-Fact → Why):** Why is it so hard for an adult to learn a new language, when young children have no problem learning multiple languages naturally?
- **Paradoxical Question (New-Fact → Why):** Why are these 2500-year-old teeth perfectly preserved after millennia in the dirt, while our teeth are practically rotting in our heads with modern dentistry?
- **The Invisible Difference:** These two dogs are the same age, eat the same food, exercise the same — yet one has grey fur and can barely hobble up the stairs, while the other plays like a puppy. What makes the difference?

### Solution-Gap
- **System/Strategy:** Use the Wild Card Copywriter system to become a highly paid copywriter.
- **Taboo Solution:** Eat this "white carb" before bed to burn fat.
- **Characterization:** Could solving "Hidden Constipation" clear up your gut problems?
- **Tiny Action:** This 1-Min Stretch Reactivates Your Metabolism.
- **Odd Object:** This 83-Page Book Allows You to Create a 7-Figure Business In Under 90 Days.
- **Blind Curiosity:** How "Quantum Linguistics" can help you master any skill in half the time.

## 2. EMOTION
Evokes a singular, powerful emotion to capture attention.

### Urgency
- **Ticking Clock:** Buy this crypto stock by September 3rd, 2024 to secure a 120x return.
- **Scarcity:** Only 100 spots left for this exclusive webinar.

### Fear
- **Warning:** WARNING: These 6 common shampoos are linked to cancer.

### Nostalgia/Hope
- **Timeline Snapshot:** Think back to when you first got your puppy. Were you excited? Nervous? Think about the joy you had when you first saw them plodding across the living room.

### Pride/Exclusivity
- Join the top 1% of entrepreneurs who have cracked the code to success.

### Guilt
- Your aging dog is depending on you.

### Anger
- Big Tech just stole your data.

## 3. SENSE-MAKING

### Secret Knowledge
- **Insider Confession:** Ex-Banker reveals weird "credit loophole."
- **Secret Society:** Yale Secret Society's 10-Sec Manifestation Trick.
- **Forgotten Wisdom:** Ancient Egyptian "Candy" Flushes Out Stuck Poop.

### Analogies & Metaphors
- **Surprise Connection:** Take a look at this "goldfish." In a minute, I'm going to tell you how this "goldfish" can help you double your close rate.
- **Natural Metaphor:** This is the Verzasca River. And if you know anything about joints, this is how they're SUPPOSED to look.
- **Mechanical Metaphor:** Weird "Pressure Valve" fixes high BP.
- **Object Metaphor:** The "Hamburger Sales Letter" is the simplest, most effective framework for writing million-dollar VSLs.

### Event/Scenario
- **Known Historical:** The "Civil War" in Your Joints.
- **Unknown Historical:** How Taft's bathroom can help you write better upsells.
- **Known Current:** What [this week's big headline] teaches us about follow-up.
- **Unknown Current:** My newborn's genius "sales pitch."
- **Known Future:** The "Singularity" of Direct Response.
- **Unknown Future:** What My Future Wedding Taught Me About Crypto.

### Storytelling
- **Story:** The 200-Year-Old "Court Ruling" That Changed Marketing Forever.
- **PIG (Punch-In-the-Gut):** "Look, the forklift just drove through the showroom window!"
- **Q&A:** Ask the Expert: Your Top 10 Keto Questions Answered.
- **Listicle:** The 5 Best Superfoods for Thyroid.
- **Comparisons:** Email vs. Text: Which One Gets Your Leads to Reply?

## 4. PATTERN INTERRUPT
Contrarian, bold, and borderline-taboo statements that force attention.

### Contrarian Claim
- **Small Belief:** Constipated? Stop eating broccoli.
- **Big Belief:** Why AI Will Never Write Good Copy.
- **System/Philosophy:** The Disastrous Failure of Keto.
- **Person/Group:** Why the Hustle Gurus Are Wrong About Your Mornings.

### Soundbite Stunner
- "You're the Bottleneck." (Then pivot into a story so it's framed, not directed at the reader.)

### Bold Proclamation
- Why You Will Never Scale on Referrals Alone.

### Controversial Opinion
- Why I Ban Freebie Seekers From My Email List.

### Elephant in the Room
- The Dark Side of the Self-Help Industry.

## 5. GIFT

### Hack/Tip
- My #1 Pickup Line (Works on Autopilot).

### Tangible Gift
- Steal our 9-figure copy-checklist (free).

## 6. INTERACTIVE

### Quiz
- **Diagnostic:** Do you have "toxic liver"?
- **Good/Bad Things:** [Quiz] Which vegetable KILLS your t-levels?

### Challenge
- Can You Spot the 5 Mistakes in This Email Marketing Campaign?

### Poll
- Vote Now: What's Your Biggest Struggle with Weight Loss?

### Look Closer
- Look closely at this ancient Egyptian hieroglyphic. Hidden in this photo is a constipation solution so powerful it recently won a Nobel Prize.

## 7. SOCIAL/TOPICAL

### Breaking News
- BREAKING NEWS: Google's AI Bombshell.

### Virality
- This "eye serum" sold out in Target in 42 seconds.

### Controversy/Gossip
- I just reported Stefan Georgi to the FTC.

### Great Experiment
- I tested 14,356 of the best skincare products.

### Social Proof
- Over 8k+ women lost fat with this.

### Case-Study
- How This Single Mom Lost 50 Pounds in 90 Days.

### New Discovery
- Harvard scientists discover new fat-switch.

### Authority
- What a 9-figure CEO just cut from their calendar?

### Memes & Zeitgeist
- "[This month's viral meme] client outreach."

## Frame-to-Page Matching

Use these defaults as a starting point — adjust based on the user's specific funnel goal:

| Page Type | Lead Frame | Secondary Frames |
|-----------|------------|------------------|
| Cold-traffic landing page | Curiosity (Information-Gap) | Pattern Interrupt, Social/Topical |
| Opt-in page | Gift, Curiosity (Solution-Gap) | Interactive (Quiz/Diagnostic) |
| Sales page (top of page) | Pattern Interrupt or Curiosity | Emotion, Sense-Making |
| Sales page (proof section) | Social/Topical (Case-Study, Authority) | Sense-Making (Storytelling) |
| Sales page (close) | Emotion (Urgency, Scarcity) | — |
| Application page | Pattern Interrupt (qualifying language) | — |
| Thank you page | Curiosity (tease next step) | Gift |
| Replay page | Social/Topical (Authority, Social Proof) | Emotion (Urgency for expiry) |
| Holding page (pre-event) | Emotion (Anticipation) | Curiosity |

## Key Principles

- **Tangible curiosity beats abstract curiosity.** Always link the curiosity element to a tangible outcome the prospect cares about.
- **Specificity amplifies frames.** Use exact names, numbers, places — not "some studies" but "Yale's 2023 study of 1,847 adults."
- **Foreign/exotic origins amplify Sense-Making.** "Russian breathing technique" beats "breathing technique."
- **Don't fabricate.** Caricaturize and tease, but base every frame in reality.
- **Match the frame to where the buyer is in awareness.** Cold = Curiosity/Pattern Interrupt. Warm = Sense-Making/Social. Hot = Emotion/Gift.
