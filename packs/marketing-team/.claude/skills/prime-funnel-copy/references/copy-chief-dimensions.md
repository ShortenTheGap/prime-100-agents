# Copy Chief & Voice Dimensions Reference

This is the full library of dimensions the user can adjust after each page is generated. Each dimension has a clear "decrease" and "increase" definition so you know exactly what to change when the user requests an adjustment.

---

## COPY CHIEFING DIMENSIONS

### 🎯 Insight Depth
- **Decrease:** Surface-level observations, common-knowledge framing
- **Increase:** Deeper into insight vectors — go further down the chain of "why" rather than adding more points

### 🪝 Hook Strength & Curiosity
- **Decrease:** Plainer openers, less rhetorical pull
- **Increase:** More creative, tangible, intuitive hooks; pull harder from Rhetorical Frames (especially Curiosity, Pattern Interrupt)

### 🧠 Psychological Triggers
- **Decrease:** Lighter touch on emotion, identity, social pressure
- **Increase:** Lean harder into psychological triggers — fear, identity, scarcity, social proof, status

### 💰 Selling Approach
- **Decrease (Sell the click):** Create more curiosity about what's on the next page; reduce direct product-selling
- **Increase (Sell the product):** Sell the offer more directly inside the page itself

### 🗣️ Natural Language
- **Decrease:** More polished, formal, deliberate
- **Increase:** More conversational, human, off-the-cuff phrasing

### 🤖 Anti-AI Patterns
- **Decrease:** Cleaner, more polished structure
- **Increase:** More uniquely human phrasings, idioms, unexpected turns, casual asides

### ⚖️ Content Weight
- **Decrease:** Thin out dense concepts, more digestible, more white space
- **Increase:** Heavier, denser, more concept-packed

### 📜 Reading Level
- **Decrease:** Simpler vocabulary without becoming choppy or childish
- **Increase:** More sophisticated vocabulary while maintaining natural flow

### 🎵 Cadence Variety
- **Decrease:** More consistent, predictable rhythm
- **Increase:** Vary rhythm, flow, and musicality through sentence structure, punctuation, and pacing

### 📏 Length
- **Decrease:** Shorter overall
- **Increase:** Longer overall

### 📝 Sentence Length
- **Decrease:** Shorter, more direct sentences
- **Increase:** Longer, more flowing sentences with multiple elements

### 📄 Paragraph Length
- **Decrease:** Shorter, more frequent paragraphs for easy scanning
- **Increase:** Longer, more developed paragraphs with multiple ideas

## VOICE — LEVEL 1 PRIORITY

### 🌡️ Emotional Warmth
- **Decrease:** More detached, clinical, objective observer stance
- **Increase:** Warmer, more caring, empathetic validation, reader-wellbeing focus

### 🔥 Energy Level
- **Decrease:** More subdued, measured, controlled enthusiasm, fewer intensifiers
- **Increase:** More energetic, passionate, animated; punctuation emphasis (!), CAPS for intensity

### 😄 Humor Presence
- **Decrease:** More straightforward, gravity-focused, minimal humor
- **Increase:** More jokes, lightheartedness, playful asides, humorous examples

### 🎭 Humor Edge
- **Decrease (Gentler):** Self-deprecating, observational, puns
- **Increase (Edgier):** Sarcasm, satire, irony, absurdist, deadpan
- Other types available: pop culture references

### 🏆 Authority Stance
- **Decrease:** More qualification, limitation acknowledgment, tentative framing
- **Increase:** More authority claiming, certainty, credentials emphasis, expert positioning

### 📣 Directness
- **Decrease:** More implied meanings, face-saving, cushioned communication
- **Increase:** More straightforward, transparent purpose, blunt truth presentation

### ⚡ Concreteness
- **Decrease:** More conceptual, theoretical, principle-based language
- **Increase:** More specific examples, tactile descriptions, real-world applications

### 🎯 Specificity
- **Decrease:** More general categories, broad strokes, universal claims
- **Increase:** More precise details, exact figures, specific instances, narrow focus

## VOICE — LEVEL 2 PRIORITY

### 🛡️ Vulnerability & Intimacy
- **Decrease:** More self-protective, professional boundaries, emotional distance
- **Increase:** More personal disclosure, emotional risk-taking, authentic sharing

### 🔤 Jargon Density
- **Decrease:** More widely understood terms, minimal industry terminology, explained concepts
- **Increase:** More specialized terminology, field-specific shorthand, technical expressions

### 🔄 Transitions & Openers
- **Pattern interrupts:** "But wait—", "Plot twist:"
- **Engaging openers:** "Imagine...", "Here's the thing..."
- **Clean transitions:** "However", "Additionally"
(Note: pattern interrupts are powerful but the listed examples are AI-tell flagged — see anti-ai.md. Adapt with the user's voice.)

### 🛡️ Hedging & Modal Words
- **Decrease:** More absolute statements, fewer qualifiers, strong assertions
- **Increase:** More hedging language (perhaps, might, tends to), cautious claims, nuanced positions

### ❓ Question Frequency
- **Decrease:** More declarative statements, fewer questions, telling approach
- **Increase:** More rhetorical questions, direct questions, reflective prompts, curiosity-builders

### 📢 Connotative Loading
- **Decrease:** More neutral, objective terms, emotionally balanced language
- **Increase:** More emotionally charged words, evocative terms, strong associations

### 😎 Emoji Usage
- **Decrease:** Minimal or no emojis, traditional text-focused approach
- **Increase:** More emojis throughout, used strategically for personality and emotional cues

### 🧠 Meta-Awareness
Demonstrates self-awareness by occasionally acknowledging what the writing is doing. Shows awareness of the selling process, the reader's potential skepticism, or the conventions being used. Creates authenticity through transparency. Don't overdo it (becomes heavy-handed) but use strategic moments of "breaking character" to build trust. Effective meta-awareness feels like a knowing wink to the reader.

### 🎭 Fourth Wall Breaks
Directly addresses the reader's probable thoughts or reactions within the copy. Anticipates objections, acknowledges unusual claims, or comments on the format itself. Should feel like a genuine connection point rather than a formulaic aside. Avoid predictable phrases like "you're probably thinking..." in favor of more subtle, personalized acknowledgments.

## ADJUSTMENT MENU FORMATTING (REQUIRED)

When presenting this menu to the user after each page, follow these rules:

1. Use clear visual separation with horizontal lines and whitespace, no emoji
2. Use proper hierarchical headings (# for main, ## for subheadings)
3. Bold formatting on dimension names (e.g., **Emotional Warmth**)
4. Bullet points for increase/decrease descriptions
5. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere in the menu
6. Proper spacing between sections for readability
7. Visually distinct adjustment section after each page generation

The user should reply with adjustments like:
- "Deeper insight vectors"
- "Stronger hook"
- "More psychological triggers"
- "Sell the click more"
- "More natural language"
- "Lighter weight"
- "Warmer tone"
- "Edgier humor"
- etc.

When they reply with adjustments, regenerate the page with those dimensions tuned, then re-present the full menu. Loop until they say **"locked in"** to advance.
