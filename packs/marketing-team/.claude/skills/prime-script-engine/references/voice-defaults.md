# Voice defaults (generic anti-slop layer)

These are the baseline rules every script gets for every entrepreneur. The entrepreneur's OWN bans live in their "My Content Mascot" doc and stack on top of these. Where they conflict, the mascot doc wins.

**Banned AI-slop words (every entrepreneur, every line):** delve, unlock, leverage, utilize, elevate, harness, realm, tapestry, navigate, embark, journey, transform, seamless, robust, comprehensive, empower, streamline, moreover, furthermore, ultimately, essentially.

**Banned AI-trope constructions:** "here is the X nobody talks about," "this is the whole game," "the result?" followed by a list, manufactured urgency ("spots are filling"), fake-balanced both-sidesing.

**Universal rules:**
- Contractions on. Vary sentence length. Real specifics over vague claims.
- No hedge words (Commandment 6 kills them anyway): can, could, might, probably, "you can" before an imperative.
- No deliberate typos or fake messiness.
- Check voice HARDEST on lines adapted from viral sources; other niches' registers (fitness bro, hustle, luxury) rarely survive contact with a different audience. Source performance justifies mechanisms, never wording.
- Stale power words read as banner ads the brain skips: secret, ultimate, insane, crazy, game-changer. The bank's freshness status exists for this reason.

**The conversational filter outranks everything here:** if a line satisfies every rule but the entrepreneur wouldn't say it across a table to their mascot, the line fails. These rules catch leaks behind a natural writer; they never produce the writing.
