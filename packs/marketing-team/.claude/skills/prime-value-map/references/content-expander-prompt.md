# Prime Content Expander — Framework

Take the sub-topics and challenges from the Value Map and expand each one into 10 deeper, more specific content ideas. The goal is to produce a massive library of highly targeted content topics the user can draw from for weeks or months.

## How It Works

The Value Map produces 5 milestones, each with 3 sub-topics = 15 sub-topics total.

For each of those 15 sub-topics, generate 10 sub-sub-topics. That's up to 150 specific, actionable content ideas.

## Guidelines for Each Sub-Sub-Topic

Every content idea must:

1. **Tie into a psychological driver** — Connect to a pain point, desired outcome, belief, objection, emotion, or old promise the target audience has been exposed to. Don't generate random topics. Every idea should hit a nerve or spark a desire.

2. **Address profound desires or fears** — Speak to the deepest motivations of the market. What keeps them up at night? What do they daydream about? The best content ideas live at the intersection of fear and aspiration.

3. **Solve a specific problem** — Each idea should be actionable enough that someone could read a post about it and actually do something different. "How to improve your marketing" is too vague. "The 3-sentence email template that rebooks ghosted clients" is specific.

4. **Be thought-provoking** — The best content ideas make people stop scrolling because they challenge an assumption, reveal a hidden truth, or reframe a problem in a way the audience hasn't considered.

5. **Provide genuine value** — Every topic should be something the target audience would genuinely benefit from consuming. Not filler. Not fluff. Real insight.

## Formatting

- Organize everything numerically for easy reference
- Group by milestone and sub-topic so the user can navigate easily
- Use this structure:

```
Milestone 1: [Title]
  Sub-topic 1.1: [Title]
    1. [Content idea]
    2. [Content idea]
    ... through 10

  Sub-topic 1.2: [Title]
    1. [Content idea]
    ... through 10

  Sub-topic 1.3: [Title]
    1. [Content idea]
    ... through 10

Milestone 2: [Title]
  ...
```

- Use the maximum length needed — do not truncate or skip topics due to length
- Every sub-topic must get its full 10 ideas

## Quality Check

Before delivering, scan the full list and verify:
- No duplicate or near-duplicate ideas across sub-topics
- Each idea is specific enough to become a standalone post
- The ideas span different angles (how-to, myth-busting, case study, framework, mistake-to-avoid, comparison, personal story, data-driven, contrarian take, step-by-step)
- The collection as a whole would position someone as a genuine authority in their space
